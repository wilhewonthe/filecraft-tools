How to Split PDF Files

Upload your PDF, choose a mode (extract specific pages, split into separate files, or extract a range), and download the result. All processing is local to your browser.

Split Modes

Extract specific pages: Enter page numbers like "1,3,5-7" to extract those pages into one PDF. Split into separate PDFs: Create one PDF per page or group of pages. Extract page range: Pull out a continuous range like pages 5-10.

All tools run entirely in your browser — your files are never uploaded.
