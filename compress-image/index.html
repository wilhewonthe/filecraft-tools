How Image Compression Works

FileCraft uses browser-native compression algorithms to reduce image file sizes. For JPEG and WebP, we apply lossy compression at your chosen quality level. For PNG, we optimize the lossless encoding. The result: significantly smaller files with minimal visible quality difference.

Why Compress Images?

  • Faster websites: Smaller images load faster and improve SEO rankings
  • Lower bandwidth: Save mobile data and hosting costs
  • Email attachments: Fit more photos under size limits
  • Social media: Meet upload limits without losing photos

Privacy First

Unlike other online compressors, FileCraft never uploads your images. Every pixel is processed locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photos stay on your device — always.
